Tonnetz Relationships

Parallel (P)

Same root, switch major↔minor. Share 2 notes. E.g. C major → C minor (C,E,G → C,E♭,G)

Relative (R)

Share 2 notes; move one note by whole step. E.g. C major → A minor (C,E,G → A,C,E)

Leading-tone (L)

Share 2 notes; move one note by semitone. E.g. C major → E minor (C,E,G → B,E,G)

Subdominant (S) / Dominant (D)

Fifth relationships: move up (dominant, V) or down (subdominant, IV) a perfect fifth.
